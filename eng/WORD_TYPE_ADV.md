# Adverbs: the next ten lessons

A plan for Adverbs 2 to Adverbs 11, in the order to teach them. Each lesson has 8 pictures
and 16 clips, numbered the same way as Verbs 1: the word alone (odd number), then its sentence (even).
Adverbs are orange on every page.

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## The order

| Lesson | Title | What is new | Frame | Status |

| 6 | How Often? | always, sometimes, never | The ___ always ___s ___. | page made (`eng/adverbs/adverbs_6.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 7 | How Much? | a little, a lot, very, too | The ___ is ___ing ___. / The ___ is ___ ___. | page made (`eng/adverbs/adverbs_7.html`); pictures and audio to do |

Lessons 2 to 5 add words and keep the grammar the children know. Lessons 6 to 11 each add one new
idea: where the word goes, how strong it is, how it differs from a describing word, comparing,
asking, and telling things in order. Adverbs 1 is listed in `WORD_TYPE_LESSONS.md`.

## Adverbs 4: Where?

Frame: **The ___ is ___ing ___.** New: the last word says where, not how.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | outside | 01 | The girl is playing outside. | 02 |
| 2 | inside | 03 | The boy is reading inside. | 04 |
| 3 | up | 05 | The cat is climbing up. | 06 |
| 4 | down | 07 | The cat is climbing down. | 08 |
| 5 | here | 09 | The boy is standing here. | 10 |
| 6 | there | 11 | The girl is standing there. | 12 |
| 7 | away | 13 | The bird is flying away. | 14 |
| 8 | back | 15 | The dog is coming back. | 16 |

Pictures: here = close to the viewer, large; there = far off, small, someone pointing. Up and down
need an arrow or a clear head direction on the same tree. These differ from Place Words 1 (in, on,
under), which need a thing after them.

## Adverbs 5: When?

Frame: **The ___ is ___ing ___.**, plus the past and "going to" from Verbs 10 to 12.
New: the last word says when.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | now | 01 | The boy is eating now. | 02 |
| 2 | today | 03 | The girl is going to school today. | 04 |
| 3 | tonight | 05 | The boy is going to read tonight. | 06 |
| 4 | yesterday | 07 | The girl cooked rice yesterday. | 08 |
| 5 | tomorrow | 09 | The boy is going to swim tomorrow. | 10 |
| 6 | soon | 11 | The bus is coming soon. | 12 |
| 7 | later | 13 | The girl is going to play later. | 14 |
| 8 | still | 15 | The boy is still sleeping. | 16 |

Pictures: the calendar marks of Verbs 10 to 12 for yesterday, today and tomorrow; a clock or the
moon for tonight; a thought bubble for later. "still" goes in front of the -ing word: the one
sentence where the word is not last. Teach after Verbs 12.

As built (lessons 5 to 7): "Make the sentence" never offers a word that is also true of the picture
(now / today, soon / later, always / every day, very / completely).

## Adverbs 6: How Often?

Frame: **The ___ always ___s ___.** New: these words go in FRONT of the action, and the action
takes -s (Verbs 8).

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | always | 01 | The boy always brushes his teeth. | 02 |
| 2 | usually | 03 | The girl usually walks to school. | 04 |
| 3 | often | 05 | The boy often plays football. | 06 |
| 4 | sometimes | 07 | The girl sometimes cooks rice. | 08 |
| 5 | never | 09 | The cat never swims. | 10 |
| 6 | every day | 11 | The girl studies every day. | 12 |
| 7 | once | 13 | The boy jumps once. | 14 |
| 8 | twice | 15 | The girl jumps twice. | 16 |

Pictures: a strip of seven days under each picture with ticks: always 7, usually 5, often 4,
sometimes 2, never 0 (all crossed), every day 7. Once and twice: one or two jump marks.
"every day", "once" and "twice" go at the end; the page must allow both positions.

## Adverbs 7: How Much?

Frames: **The ___ is ___ing ___.** and **The ___ is ___ ___.** New: words that make an action
or a describing word stronger or weaker. The pictures come in pairs.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a little | 01 | The boy is eating a little. | 02 |
| 2 | a lot | 03 | The boy is eating a lot. | 04 |
| 3 | very | 05 | The tea is very hot. | 06 |
| 4 | too | 07 | The tea is too hot. | 08 |
| 5 | almost | 09 | The cup is almost full. | 10 |
| 6 | completely | 11 | The cup is completely full. | 12 |
| 7 | more | 13 | The girl is drinking more. | 14 |
| 8 | less | 15 | The boy is drinking less. | 16 |

Pictures: very hot = steam, the girl blows and drinks; too hot = she cannot drink, tongue out.
More and less: the girl and the boy side by side, a big glass and a small glass, the one meant
marked (the mark of Adjectives 9). Teach after Adjectives 8 (Very).

## Adverbs 8: Quick or Quickly?

Frames: **The ___ is ___.** and **The ___ is ___ing ___.** New: the describing word tells about
the person or thing, the how word tells about the action. The pictures come in pairs.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | quick | 01 | The boy is quick. | 02 |
| 2 | quickly | 03 | The boy is running quickly. | 04 |
| 3 | careful | 05 | The girl is careful. | 06 |
| 4 | carefully | 07 | The girl is writing carefully. | 08 |
| 5 | happy | 09 | The dog is happy. | 10 |
| 6 | happily | 11 | The dog is playing happily. | 12 |
| 7 | good | 13 | The song is good. | 14 |
| 8 | well | 15 | The girl is singing well. | 16 |

Page: the describing word in green, the how word in orange, and "quick → quickly" under the word.
Spelling to point out: happy → happ**i**ly. good → well changes completely.
"Make the sentence" offers both words of the pair, so the child must choose by the frame.
Each pair can share one picture: the two sentences are both true of it.

## Adverbs 10: How? Where? When?

Frame: **How is the ___ ___ing? ___.** New: the questions these words answer. All words are known.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How? | 01 | How is the boy walking? Slowly. | 02 |
| 2 | How? | 03 | How is the girl singing? Well. | 04 |
| 3 | Where? | 05 | Where is the girl playing? Outside. | 06 |
| 4 | Where? | 07 | Where is the cat climbing? Up. | 08 |
| 5 | When? | 09 | When is the boy going to swim? Tomorrow. | 10 |
| 6 | When? | 11 | When did the girl cook rice? Yesterday. | 12 |
| 7 | How often? | 13 | How often does the boy brush his teeth? Always. | 14 |
| 8 | How much? | 15 | How much is the boy eating? A lot. | 16 |

Page: a different build from the others. The child hears the question and taps the answer from
four how / where / when words; "Make the sentence" becomes "Pick the question word". Record the
question and the answer in two voices if possible. Pictures can be reused from lessons 1 to 7.

As built: step 2 is "Tap the answer", step 3 is "Which question?", and there is no "Tap the picture".
"Yes or no?" asks whether the answer fits the question, so the page needs 8 more clips, the question with an
answer of another kind (clip = picture number + 16):

| Clip | Sentence | Clip | Sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17 | How is the boy walking? Tomorrow. | 18 | How is the girl singing? Outside. |
| 19 | Where is the girl playing? Slowly. | 20 | Where is the cat climbing? Yesterday. |
| 21 | When is the boy going to swim? Up. | 22 | When did the girl cook rice? Well. |
| 23 | How often does the boy brush his teeth? Outside. | 24 | How much is the boy eating? Always. |

## Adverbs 11: First, Then, Finally

Frame: **First the ___ ___s ___. Then she ___s ___.** New: words that put actions in order.
The eight pictures tell one girl's day, in order.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | first | 01 | First the girl wakes up. | 02 |
| 2 | then | 03 | Then she washes her face. | 04 |
| 3 | next | 05 | Next she eats breakfast. | 06 |
| 4 | after that | 07 | After that she goes to school. | 08 |
| 5 | later | 09 | Later she plays with her friends. | 10 |
| 6 | soon | 11 | Soon she comes home. | 12 |
| 7 | finally | 13 | Finally she goes to bed. | 14 |
| 8 | again | 15 | The next day she wakes up again. | 16 |

Page: add a step where the child puts the eight pictures in order (the "order" question of the
story quizzes). "Tap the picture" works only with the whole sentence, since "then" alone fits
several pictures. "later" and "soon" are known from Adverbs 5. Teach after Verbs 8 and Pronouns 1 ("she").

As built: five steps, with "Put in order" third. In "Make the sentence" the gap is the action; "First" and
"Finally" are gaps too, but then / next / after that are given, since any of them would be right.
