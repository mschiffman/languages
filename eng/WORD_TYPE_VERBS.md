# Verbs: the next ten lessons

A plan for Verbs 3 to Verbs 12, in the order to teach them. Each lesson has 8 pictures and
16 clips, numbered the same way as Verbs 1: the word alone (odd number), then its sentence (even).

Last updated: 2026-11-15

Lessons 3 to 6 add words and keep the grammar of Verbs 1. Lessons 7 to 12 keep mostly known
words and add one new piece of grammar each. Lessons 13 to 15 are everyday activities: new words,
the grammar of Verbs 4. They can be taught any time after lesson 6. Verbs 1 and 2 are listed in `WORD_TYPE_LESSONS.md`.

## Verbs 10: Yesterday 1

Frame: **`Yesterday the ___ ___ed ___.`** New: the past with -ed. All verbs are known from lessons 1 to 6.

| Pic | Word    | Word clip | Sentence                             | Sentence clip |
| --- | ------- | --------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------- |
| 1   | walked  | 01        | Yesterday the girl walked to school. | 02            |
| 2   | jumped  | 03        | Yesterday the girl jumped.           | 04            |
| 3   | played  | 05        | Yesterday the boy played football.   | 06            |
| 4   | cooked  | 07        | Yesterday the girl cooked rice.      | 08            |
| 5   | washed  | 09        | Yesterday the boy washed his hands.  | 10            |
| 6   | cleaned | 11        | Yesterday the boy cleaned the table. | 12            |
| 7   | painted | 13        | Yesterday the girl painted a flower. | 14            |
| 8   | climbed | 15        | Yesterday the boy climbed the tree.  | 16            |

Pictures: a picture alone cannot show the past. Give every picture the same "yesterday" mark,
for example a calendar in the corner with yesterday's square coloured. The same mark is used in
lesson 11, and a "tomorrow" mark in lesson 12.
Recording: the three sounds of -ed should be clear: walk**t**, play**d**, paint**id**.
