# Pronouns: the next ten lessons

A plan for Pronouns 2 to Pronouns 11, in the order to teach them. Each lesson has 8 pictures
and 16 clips, numbered the same way as Verbs 1: the word alone (odd number), then its sentence (even).
Pronouns are blue on every page, like the people and things they stand for.

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Lessons 2 to 7 each add one set of words. Lessons 8 and 9 use known words to ask questions.
Lessons 10 and 11 add two last sets. Pronouns 1 is listed in `WORD_TYPE_LESSONS.md`.

For a Burmese speaker the hard part is that English keeps changing the word. Spoken Burmese says
သူ for both he and she, and makes "his", "him" and "his own" by adding an ending to the same word
(သူ့, သူ့ကို, သူ့ဟာ). English has a different word each time: he, his, him, his, himself; and she,
her, her, hers, herself. So every page keeps the boy and the girl side by side, and the pictures
use the same signs as Pronouns 1: **I** = a hand on the speaker's own chest, **you** = the speaker
points at the friend, **we** = two children with hands on their chests, **they** = two other
children a little way off.

Several pages already made use these words before they are taught here: "his teeth" (Adverbs 6),
"his hands" (Prepositions 5), "her face" and "she" (Adverbs 11), "Yes, it is." (Prepositions 8).
Lessons 2 and 3 are the ones those pages need.

## Pronouns 2: My, Your, His, Her

Frame: **It is ___ ___.** New: a word in front of the thing that says whose it is.
The frame of Adjectives 6 ("It is a big dog.") with the new word where "a big" was.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | my | 01 | It is my hat. | 02 |
| 2 | your | 03 | It is your hat. | 04 |
| 3 | his | 05 | It is his bag. | 06 |
| 4 | her | 07 | It is her bag. | 08 |
| 5 | my | 09 | It is my book. | 10 |
| 6 | your | 11 | It is your book. | 12 |
| 7 | his | 13 | It is his ball. | 14 |
| 8 | her | 15 | It is her ball. | 16 |

Pictures: 1 the girl holds the hat, her other hand on her chest; 2 the same girl points at the boy,
who holds the hat. 3 and 4: the same bag, on the boy's back and on the girl's. 5 to 8 the same
again with the boy speaking. Page: show the pair under the word, as Adverbs 1 shows quick → quickly:
I → my, you → your, he → his, she → her. No "a" or "the" with these words: not "a my hat".
"Tap the picture" works only with the whole sentence, since each word comes twice.

## Pronouns 3: I Wash My Hands

Frame: **___ am / is / are ___ing ___ ___.** New: its, our, their; and which word goes with which
person. All the actions are known.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | my | 01 | I am washing my hands. | 02 |
| 2 | your | 03 | You are reading your book. | 04 |
| 3 | his | 05 | He is kicking his ball. | 06 |
| 4 | her | 07 | She is carrying her bag. | 08 |
| 5 | its | 09 | It is eating its food. | 10 |
| 6 | our | 11 | We are cleaning our room. | 12 |
| 7 | their | 13 | They are flying their kite. | 14 |
| 8 | our | 15 | We are eating our lunch. | 16 |

Pictures: the signs of Pronouns 1 (hand on chest, pointing, two together, two others). 5 is the dog
at its bowl. Page: the word card shows the pair: I → my, we → our, they → their, it → its.
"Make the sentence" has two gaps, the person and the word that goes with it, so "He … her ball"
is there to be refused. Teacher's note: "its" has no mark; "it's" means "it is".
The actions are from Verbs 1, 4, 6 and 9. New things: hands, food, room, lunch.

## Pronouns 4: Me, You, Him, Her

Frame: **The ___ likes ___.** and **The ___ is helping ___.** New: the word changes when the
person comes AFTER the action. "you" stays the same.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | me | 01 | The dog likes me. | 02 |
| 2 | you | 03 | The dog likes you. | 04 |
| 3 | him | 05 | The dog likes him. | 06 |
| 4 | her | 07 | The dog likes her. | 08 |
| 5 | me | 09 | The teacher is helping me. | 10 |
| 6 | you | 11 | The teacher is helping you. | 12 |
| 7 | him | 13 | The girl is helping him. | 14 |
| 8 | her | 15 | The boy is helping her. | 16 |

Pictures: 1 the dog licks the girl, her hand on her chest; 2 the girl points at the boy, whom the
dog is licking; 3 and 4 the dog with the boy, the dog with the girl, nobody pointing.
Page: show the pair under the word: I → me, he → him, she → her. Add the "Swap the name" step of
Pronouns 1: "The dog likes the boy." → tap him → "The dog likes him." (4 more clips, 17 to 20:
The dog likes the boy. / The dog likes the girl. / The girl is helping the boy. / The boy is
helping the girl.) Teacher's note: "her" is two words in one: her bag (lesson 2) and likes her.
"like" is from Verbs 2, "help" from Verbs 6.
As built: five steps, with "Swap the name" third, for pictures 3, 4, 7 and 8 (clips 17 to 20, in that order).
"Make the sentence" has one gap, the word at the end.

## Pronouns 5: Us, Them, It

Frame: **The ___ is ___ing ___.** New: us and them; and "it" and "them" for things.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | us | 01 | The teacher is helping us. | 02 |
| 2 | them | 03 | The teacher is helping them. | 04 |
| 3 | us | 05 | The dog likes us. | 06 |
| 4 | them | 07 | The dog likes them. | 08 |
| 5 | it | 09 | The boy is kicking it. | 10 |
| 6 | them | 11 | The girl is carrying them. | 12 |
| 7 | it | 13 | The girl is reading it. | 14 |
| 8 | them | 15 | The boy is washing them. | 16 |

Pictures: 1 and 3 two children with hands on their chests; 2 and 4 two other children, a little
way off. 5 one ball, 6 a pile of books, 7 one book, 8 several cups: one thing = it, more than
one = them. Page: show the pair: we → us, they → them, it → it. "Swap the name" as in lesson 4:
"The boy is kicking the ball." → it; "The girl is carrying the books." → them (4 more clips,
17 to 20, for pictures 5 to 8).
As built: five steps, as lesson 4. Clips 17 to 20: The boy is kicking the ball. / The girl is carrying the books. /
The girl is reading the book. / The boy is washing the cups.

## Pronouns 6: This, That, These, Those

Frames: **This is a ___.** and **These are ___s.** New: pointing words. Near or far, one or more.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | this | 01 | This is a cat. | 02 |
| 2 | that | 03 | That is a cat. | 04 |
| 3 | these | 05 | These are cats. | 06 |
| 4 | those | 07 | Those are cats. | 08 |
| 5 | this | 09 | This is my bag. | 10 |
| 6 | that | 11 | That is your bag. | 12 |
| 7 | these | 13 | These are my shoes. | 14 |
| 8 | those | 15 | Those are his shoes. | 16 |

Pictures: the same child in all eight. Near = the thing is at the child's hand, large; far = the
child points across the picture at it, small. 1 to 4 are a square: one near, one far, three near,
three far. Page: show the square on the word card (this → these, that → those, this ↔ that).
"Make the sentence" has two gaps, the pointing word and "is a" / "are", so "These is" is there to
be refused. Needs plurals; see Prepositions 9. "Tap the picture" works only with the whole sentence.

## Pronouns 7: Mine, Yours, His, Hers

Frame: **The ___ is ___.** New: saying whose it is with no thing after the word.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | mine | 01 | The hat is mine. | 02 |
| 2 | yours | 03 | The hat is yours. | 04 |
| 3 | his | 05 | The bag is his. | 06 |
| 4 | hers | 07 | The bag is hers. | 08 |
| 5 | ours | 09 | The house is ours. | 10 |
| 6 | theirs | 11 | The house is theirs. | 12 |
| 7 | mine | 13 | The shoes are mine. | 14 |
| 8 | hers | 15 | The shoes are hers. | 16 |

Pictures: 1 to 4 can be the pictures of lesson 2. As built, 7 is the BOY with his shoes and a hand on his chest
(with the girl there, "The shoes are hers." would be true of both 7 and 8). Page: show both ways on the word card:
"my hat → mine", "her bag → hers", "his bag → his". Spelling to point out: most add -s
(your → yours, her → hers, our → ours, their → theirs); my → mine changes; his stays the same.
None has a mark: not "her's". "Tap the picture" works only with the whole sentence.
Teach after lessons 2 and 3.

| 2 | Whose? | 03 | Whose bag is it? It is hers. | 04 |
| 3 | Whose? | 05 | Whose book is it? It is mine. | 06 |
| 4 | Whose? | 07 | Whose ball is it? It is yours. | 08 |
| 5 | Whose? | 09 | Whose house is it? It is ours. | 10 |
| 6 | Whose? | 11 | Whose kite is it? It is theirs. | 12 |
| 7 | Whose? | 13 | Whose shoes are they? They are his. | 14 |
| 8 | Whose? | 15 | Whose shoes are they? They are hers. | 16 |

Page: the build of Adverbs 10 and Prepositions 8: the child hears the question and taps the answer

ours / theirs). 7 and 8: more than one thing, so "are they" and "They are".
As built: steps are Look and listen, Tap the picture (with the whole question and answer), Tap the answer, Yes or no?.

## Pronouns 9: Who? What?

Frames: **Who is ___ing? The ___.** and **What is the ___ ___ing? A ___.**
New: "who" asks about a person, "what" asks about a thing. All the other words are known.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who? | 01 | Who is running? The boy. | 02 |
| 2 | Who? | 03 | Who is singing? The girl. | 04 |
| 3 | Who? | 05 | Who is sleeping? The baby. | 06 |
| 4 | Who? | 07 | Who is helping the girl? The teacher. | 08 |
| 5 | What? | 09 | What is the boy eating? A mango. | 10 |
| 6 | What? | 11 | What is the girl carrying? A bag. | 12 |
| 7 | What? | 13 | What is the boy kicking? A ball. | 14 |
| 8 | What? | 15 | What is the girl reading? A book. | 16 |

Page: the build of Adverbs 10: "Tap the answer" (two people and two things are offered, so the
question word decides half of it), then "Which question?" (Who / What). "Yes or no?" needs 8 more
clips, clips 17 to 24: a who-question answered with a thing ("Who is running? A ball."), and a what-question
answered with the wrong thing ("What is the boy kicking? A mango."). As built, the what-questions are not
answered with a person, since "kicking the girl" is not a sentence to record. Teach after Adverbs 10, which has How / Where / When.

## Pronouns 10: Myself, Yourself

Frame: **___ am / is / are ___ing ___self.** New: the action comes back to the one who does it.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | myself | 01 | I am washing myself. | 02 |
| 2 | yourself | 03 | You are washing yourself. | 04 |
| 3 | himself | 05 | He is drying himself. | 06 |
| 4 | herself | 07 | She is looking at herself. | 08 |
| 5 | itself | 09 | It is washing itself. | 10 |
| 6 | ourselves | 11 | We are drying ourselves. | 12 |
| 7 | themselves | 13 | They are washing themselves. | 14 |
| 8 | himself | 15 | He is feeding himself. | 16 |

Pictures: 4 is the girl at a mirror; 5 is the cat licking its paw; 8 is the baby with a spoon.
Page: show how the word is made: my + self, him + self, our + selves. Spelling to point out:
one person -self, more than one -selves. The contrast to teach: "He is washing him." (another boy)
and "He is washing himself."; "Make the sentence" offers both him and himself.
The actions are from Verbs 4, 6 and 13 and Prepositions 11 ("look at"). Teach after Verbs 13
(Keeping Clean) and Pronouns 4.

## Pronouns 11: Someone, Something

Frame: **___ is ___ the ___.** and **___ is ___ing.** New: words for a person or a thing you do
not name. The pictures come in pairs.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | someone | 01 | Someone is at the door. | 02 |
| 2 | no one | 03 | No one is at the door. | 04 |
| 3 | something | 05 | Something is in the box. | 06 |
| 4 | nothing | 07 | Nothing is in the box. | 08 |
| 5 | everyone | 09 | Everyone is sitting. | 10 |
| 6 | no one | 11 | No one is sitting. | 12 |
| 7 | everything | 13 | Everything is on the table. | 14 |
| 8 | nothing | 15 | Nothing is on the table. | 16 |

Pictures: 1 a shadow behind the glass of the door, a hand knocking; 2 the same door, empty step.
3 a closed box that shakes, with a question mark; 4 the same box open and empty. 5 and 6 the same
class, all sitting and all standing. 7 and 8 the same table with all the toys on it, and bare with
the toys on the floor. Page: show how the words are made: some + one, no + thing, every + one.
-one = a person, -thing = a thing. All of them take "is", even "everyone".
"Tap the picture" works only with the whole sentence, since "no one" and "nothing" come twice.
As built: one gap with four words, never one that is also true of the picture ("Someone is sitting." when
everyone is; "No one is in the box." when nothing is).
Teach after Prepositions 10 ("at the door").

## Open questions

- Lessons 4 and 5 add a fifth step, "Swap the name", as Pronouns 1 has. Keep it, or four steps like the other new pages?
- Lessons 6, 7 and 8 use plurals ("cats", "shoes", "are they"). The same question as in the prepositions plan: is there a lesson for -s on things?
- Lesson 3 could come first. It uses a frame the children already know and shows I → my side by side; lesson 2 has the simpler sentence.
- Lesson 9 could move to a "questions" set of its own with Adverbs 10 and Prepositions 8.
- "you" for more than one person (and "yourselves") is left out everywhere.
