beginner to lower-intermediate). The quiz tests the English expressions on the

What to write
- 18 questions in all: 2 match, about 7 say, about 4 judge, about 3 build, and
  reply questions only if the cards include answers to other cards (see below).
  Mix the types; do not put all of one type together, except the two match
  blocks, which come first.
- Cover as many different cards as you can. Use the cards' notes: they say
  when each phrase is used and what Burmese speakers get wrong.

Rules for every block
- A "card" means the English phrase of a card, copied EXACTLY, with its
  capital letters and punctuation ("Good evening." "What a shame!").
- The + answer, the PROMPT and the PHRASE must be cards. A wrong option (-)
  may be a card or any other phrase.
- Every wrong option and every judge block has a tag: the KIND of mistake.
  Built-in tags:
    time      wrong time of day
    moment    wrong moment (arriving / leaving, before / after, first meeting / again)
    register  too casual or too formal for this person
    literal   translated word for word from Burmese
    reply     doesn't answer what was said
    words     wrong word, word form or word order
    meaning   the phrase means something else
  If the topic needs another kind of mistake, define it under "## tags"
  (one short lowercase word = a tip). You may also give a built-in tag a new
  tip there when its usual meaning doesn't fit the topic. Leave "## tags" out
  if you need none.
- A wrong option must be clearly wrong IN THIS SCENE. If a native speaker
  could also say it here, do not use it as a wrong option. If you cannot name
  the kind of mistake, it is not a wrong option.
- Notes are short, plain English a learner can read: say why the option is
  wrong here and, if useful, when it IS used. No grammar terms.

match
- 5 cards per block, one per line, nothing else. The learner matches each
  English phrase with its Burmese.
- Never put two cards with the same or nearly the same Burmese in one block.

say
- SCENE: two or three short parts joined by " · ", each starting with one
  emoji: who, when or where, and what is happening. Example:
  SCENE: 🌙 8 pm · 🍽️ At a restaurant · You walk in.
- MY: the same scene in natural spoken Burmese (…တယ်။ style).
- Exactly one + line and two or three - lines.

reply
- Use it only when both the PROMPT and the + answer are cards.
- SCENE and MY are optional here.

build
- The learner taps the words of the + card into order. Choose a card of three
  words or more, or two words when one of them is easy to get wrong.
- Each - line is ONE extra word that makes a typical mistake (a wrong form,
  a word translated from Burmese). One or two - lines. The decoy must not make
  another correct English sentence.

judge
- The learner decides whether saying PHRASE in this scene is OK.
- OK: yes or no. Make about half of them "no".
- TAG is the kind of mistake the question is about, also when OK is yes.
- NOTE is needed when OK is no.

Do not add anything else: no numbering, no comments, no blank "TODO" lines.
