# Prepositions: the next ten lessons

A plan for Prepositions 2 to Prepositions 11, in the order to teach them. Each lesson has 8 pictures
and 16 clips, numbered the same way as Verbs 1: the word alone (odd number), then its sentence (even).
Prepositions are purple on every page.

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## The order

| Lesson | Title | What is new | Frame | Status |

| 6 | With and Without | words that are not about place or time | The ___ is ___ing ___ ___. | page made (`eng/prepositions/prepositions_6.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 7 | Is Not | saying no, with known words | The ___ is not ___ the ___. | page made (`eng/prepositions/prepositions_7.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 8 | Where Is It? | asking and answering | Where is the ___? ___ the ___. | page made (`eng/prepositions/prepositions_8.html`); pictures and audio to do |

Lessons 2 to 6 add words and keep the grammar the children know. Lessons 7 to 11 keep mostly known
words and add one new idea each: saying no, asking, "there is", choosing between in / on / at, and
the small word that belongs to an action. Prepositions 1 is listed in `WORD_TYPE_LESSONS.md`.

For a Burmese speaker the hard part is not the meaning but the place of the word and the choice.
Burmese puts the place word AFTER the thing (ပုံးအထဲမှာ, "box inside-at"), and one ending, မှာ,
covers in, on and at. So every page shows the English order, and lesson 10 is about the choice.

## Prepositions 2: More Place Words

Frame: **The ___ is ___ the ___.** Nothing new but the words. Three opposite pairs and two words alone.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | above | 01 | The bird is above the tree. | 02 |
| 2 | below | 03 | The fish is below the boat. | 04 |
| 3 | near | 05 | The dog is near the house. | 06 |
| 4 | far from | 07 | The dog is far from the house. | 08 |
| 5 | inside | 09 | The cat is inside the house. | 10 |
| 6 | outside | 11 | The cat is outside the house. | 12 |
| 7 | between | 13 | The ball is between the boxes. | 14 |
| 8 | against | 15 | The bike is against the wall. | 16 |

Pictures: the same dog and house in 3 and 4, the same cat and house in 5 and 6; only the position
changes. "above" is not touching (compare "on" in lesson 1): the bird is in the air.
"inside" and "outside" are in Adverbs 4 without a thing after them ("The girl is playing outside.");
here they take one. "between" needs two things: "the boxes" is the first plural on these pages.
New things: boat, house, wall; fish.

## Prepositions 3: Into and Out Of

Frame: **The ___ is ___ing ___ the ___.** New: the word says where something is GOING, not where
it is. The pictures come in opposite pairs.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | into | 01 | The cat is jumping into the box. | 02 |
| 2 | out of | 03 | The cat is jumping out of the box. | 04 |
| 3 | onto | 05 | The cat is jumping onto the table. | 06 |
| 4 | off | 07 | The cat is jumping off the table. | 08 |
| 5 | up | 09 | The boy is walking up the hill. | 10 |
| 6 | down | 11 | The boy is walking down the hill. | 12 |
| 7 | to | 13 | The girl is walking to the school. | 14 |
| 8 | from | 15 | The girl is walking from the school. | 16 |

Pictures: each pair is the same drawing with the movement turned round; an arrow shows the way.
Teacher's note: in = where it is, into = where it is going ("The cat is in the box." / "The cat is
jumping into the box."). The same for on / onto. "up" and "down" are in Adverbs 4 without a thing
after them. "to school" without "the" is in Verbs 5; with "the" it is this school, the building.
New things: hill.

## Prepositions 5: At, On, In: When?

Frame: **The ___ ___s ___ ___.** New: at, on and in also say WHEN; and before, after, until.
The action takes -s (Verbs 8). Teach after Verbs 8.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | at | 01 | The boy gets up at six. | 02 |
| 2 | at | 03 | The girl sleeps at night. | 04 |
| 3 | on | 05 | The girl swims on Sunday. | 06 |
| 4 | in | 07 | The boy reads in the morning. | 08 |
| 5 | in | 09 | The girl cooks in the evening. | 10 |
| 6 | before | 11 | The boy washes his hands before lunch. | 12 |
| 7 | after | 13 | The girl plays after school. | 14 |
| 8 | until | 15 | The boy sleeps until seven. | 16 |

Rule to show on the page: **at** + a clock time (and "at night"), **on** + a day, **in** + a part
of the day. Pictures: a clock for 1 and 8, a calendar with the day marked for 3, the sun low on the
left for the morning and low on the right with a red sky for the evening, the moon for night.
"Tap the picture" works only with the whole sentence, since "at" and "in" each come twice.
This lesson needs the clock times and the days of the week; if the children do not know them yet,
teach "six", "seven" and "Sunday" as part of it.
As built: "Make the sentence" offers at / on / in for 1 to 5 and three chosen words for 6 to 8.

## Prepositions 6: With and Without

Frame: **The ___ is ___ing ___ ___.** New: small words that are not about place or time.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | with | 01 | The boy is walking with an umbrella. | 02 |
| 2 | without | 03 | The boy is walking without an umbrella. | 04 |
| 3 | for | 05 | The present is for the girl. | 06 |
| 4 | from | 07 | The present is from the boy. | 08 |
| 5 | by | 09 | The girl is going to school by bus. | 10 |
| 6 | by | 11 | The boy is going home by boat. | 12 |
| 7 | about | 13 | The book is about a dog. | 14 |
| 8 | of | 15 | The cup is full of tea. | 16 |

Pictures: 1 and 2 in the rain, the same boy dry and wet. 3 and 4 are one drawing, the boy handing
the present to the girl, with the one meant marked (the mark of Adjectives 9). "by" + a vehicle has
no "a" or "the". "from" is known from lesson 3 as a movement word; here it says who gave it.
New things: umbrella, present, boat.
As built: "Make the sentence" offers four small words chosen for each picture, and "Tap the picture" uses the
whole sentence. In "Yes or no?" pictures 3 and 4 get the sentence of picture 7 or 8, since "for the girl" and
"from the boy" are both true of their drawing.

## Prepositions 8: Where Is It?

Frame: **Where is the ___? ___ the ___.** New: the question. All place words are known.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where? | 01 | Where is the cat? In the box. | 02 |
| 2 | Where? | 03 | Where is the dog? Under the car. | 04 |
| 3 | Where? | 05 | Where is the ball? Behind the tree. | 06 |
| 4 | Where? | 07 | Where is the bird? Above the house. | 08 |
| 5 | Is it? | 09 | Is the cat on the table? Yes, it is. | 10 |
| 6 | Is it? | 11 | Is the cat on the table? No, it is not. | 12 |
| 7 | Is it? | 13 | Is the ball next to the box? Yes, it is. | 14 |
| 8 | Is it? | 15 | Is the ball next to the box? No, it is not. | 16 |

Page: the build of Adverbs 10 (`eng/adverbs/adverbs_10.html`): the child hears the question and
taps the answer. For 1 to 4 the answers offered are four place phrases about the same two things;
for 5 to 8 they are "Yes, it is." and "No, it is not." Record the question and the answer in two
voices if possible. "it" is in Pronouns 1. Teach after Prepositions 2 and 7.

As built: steps are Look and listen, Tap the picture (with the whole question and answer), Tap the answer,
Yes or no?. For 5 to 8 "Yes or no?" uses the partner's clip; for 1 to 4 it needs 4 more clips, the question
with a wrong place (clip = picture number + 16):

|---|---|---|---|
| 17 | Where is the cat? On the box. | 18 | Where is the dog? In the car. |
| 19 | Where is the ball? In front of the tree. | 20 | Where is the bird? Behind the house. |

## Prepositions 9: There Is, There Are

Frame: **There is a ___ ___ the ___.** New: saying what is in a place, and "there are" for more
than one. The pictures come in pairs: one, then two.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | there is | 01 | There is a cat in the box. | 02 |
| 2 | there are | 03 | There are two cats in the box. | 04 |
| 3 | there is | 05 | There is a bird on the wall. | 06 |
| 4 | there are | 07 | There are two birds on the wall. | 08 |
| 5 | there is | 09 | There is a ball under the table. | 10 |
| 6 | there are | 11 | There are two balls under the table. | 12 |
| 7 | there is | 13 | There is a dog behind the tree. | 14 |
| 8 | there are | 15 | There are two dogs behind the tree. | 16 |

Page: "Make the sentence" has three gaps: "There is a" / "There are two" (amber), the thing, the
place word. Spelling to point out: cat → cat**s**. This is the first lesson with plurals as its
subject; if the children have not met -s on things yet, teach it here with "two".
"Tap the picture" works only with the whole sentence.

## Prepositions 10: In, On or At?

Frame: **The ___ is ___ ___.** New: which of the three goes with which kind of place.
No new place words.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | in | 01 | The boy is in the kitchen. | 02 |
| 2 | in | 03 | The fish is in the river. | 04 |
| 3 | on | 05 | The picture is on the wall. | 06 |
| 4 | on | 07 | The book is on the floor. | 08 |
| 5 | on | 09 | The girl is on the bus. | 10 |
| 6 | at | 11 | The girl is at school. | 12 |
| 7 | at | 13 | The boy is at home. | 14 |
| 8 | at | 15 | The woman is at the market. | 16 |

Rule to show on the page: **in** = inside something with sides or edges (a room, a river);
**on** = touching a surface (a wall, a floor), and a bus, a boat, a bike; **at** = a place where you
go to do something (school, home, the market). Burmese uses မှာ for all three, so this is where the
children will make most mistakes. "Make the sentence" has one gap, the small word, with the three
choices. "Tap the picture" works only with the whole sentence. "at school" and "at home" have no "the".
The hardest lesson of the set; it could be split in two (in / on, then at) if it proves too much.
New things: kitchen, floor, picture; woman.
As built: "Yes or no?" never puts "The boy is at home." under the kitchen picture, or the other way round.

## Prepositions 11: Look At, Listen To

Frame: **The ___ is ___ing ___ the ___.** New: some actions always take the same small word, and
it has to be learned with the action. All the actions are known except where marked.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | look at | 01 | The boy is looking at the bird. | 02 |
| 2 | listen to | 03 | The girl is listening to the teacher. | 04 |
| 3 | point at | 05 | The boy is pointing at the kite. | 06 |
| 4 | wait for | 07 | The girl is waiting for the bus. | 08 |
| 5 | think about | 09 | The boy is thinking about the mangoes. | 10 |
| 6 | play with | 11 | The girl is playing with the ball. | 12 |
| 7 | go to | 13 | The boy is going to the market. | 14 |
| 8 | come from | 15 | The girl is coming from the market. | 16 |

Page: the action in red and its small word in purple, shown together on the word card ("look at").
"Make the sentence" has two gaps, the action and the small word, and always offers "at", "to",
"for" and "with", so "listening at" and "waiting to" are there to be refused.
The actions are from Verbs 3, 5, 8 and 14. Teach after Verbs 14.
As built: four small words are offered, chosen for each picture, and never one that would also be right
("pointing to the kite", "looking for the bird").
New things: kite.

## Open questions

- Lesson 5 needs clock times and days. Are they taught somewhere before it?
- Lessons 2 and 9 use plurals ("the boxes", "two cats"). Is there a lesson for -s on things?
- "toward" (lesson 4) is the American form; British English says "towards". The audio voice is British.
- Lesson 8 mixes two question types. It could be two lessons: "Where is it?" and "Is it …?".
