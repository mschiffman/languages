# Adjectives: the next ten lessons

A plan for Adjectives 2 to Adjectives 11, in the order to teach them. Each lesson has 8 pictures
and 16 clips, numbered the same way as Verbs 1: the word alone (odd number), then its sentence (even).

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## The order

| Lesson | Title | What is new | Frame | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Describing Words | big, small, long, short, happy, sad, hot, cold | The ___ is ___. | page made (`eng/adjectives/adjectives_1.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 2 | More Opposites | 4 more opposite pairs | The ___ is ___. | page made (`eng/adjectives/adjectives_2.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 3 | Colours | 8 colours | The ___ is ___. | page made (`eng/adjectives/adjectives_3.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 4 | How Do You Feel? | 8 feelings | The ___ is ___. | page made (`eng/adjectives/adjectives_4.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 5 | Touch and Taste | what you feel with your hands and mouth | The ___ is ___. | page made (`eng/adjectives/adjectives_5.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 6 | A Big Dog | the describing word in front of the thing | It is a ___ ___. | page made (`eng/adjectives/adjectives_6.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 7 | Is Not | saying no, with known words | The ___ is not ___. | page made (`eng/adjectives/adjectives_7.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 8 | Very | making the word stronger | The ___ is very ___. | page made (`eng/adjectives/adjectives_8.html`); pictures and audio to do |
| 9 | Bigger Than | comparing two things with -er | The ___ is ___er than the ___. | page made (`eng/adjectives/adjectives_9.html`); pictures and audio to do |

| 11 | Good, Better, Best | forms that change; "more" and "most" | The ___ is ___. | page made (`eng/adjectives/adjectives_11.html`); pictures and audio to do |

Lessons 2 to 5 add words and keep the grammar of Adjectives 1. Lessons 6 to 11 keep mostly known
words and add one new piece of grammar each. Adjectives 1 is listed in `WORD_TYPE_LESSONS.md`.

## Adjectives 9: Bigger Than

Frame: **The ___ is ___er than the ___.** New: comparing two things. Each picture shows both things side by side.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | bigger | 01 | The elephant is bigger than the dog. | 02 |
| 2 | smaller | 03 | The mouse is smaller than the cat. | 04 |
| 3 | longer | 05 | The snake is longer than the pencil. | 06 |
| 4 | shorter | 07 | The pencil is shorter than the snake. | 08 |
| 5 | taller | 09 | The man is taller than the boy. | 10 |
| 6 | faster | 11 | The dog is faster than the snail. | 12 |
| 7 | slower | 13 | The snail is slower than the dog. | 14 |
| 8 | older | 15 | The man is older than the boy. | 16 |

Page: show the pair beside the word (big → bigger), as Adverbs 1 shows quick → quickly.
Spelling to point out: big → bi**gg**er. "Make the sentence" has three gaps: thing, -er word, thing.
Pictures: the thing the sentence is about (the first one) carries a mark, the same in every picture.
So 3 + 4 and 6 + 7 are the same drawing with the mark moved. 5 and 8 show the same man and boy:
a height chart in 5, birthday candles in 8.

The page also needs 8 more clips for "Yes or no?", the same sentences turned round (clip = picture number + 16):

| 17 | The dog is bigger than the elephant. | 18 | The cat is smaller than the mouse. |
| 19 | The pencil is longer than the snake. | 20 | The snake is shorter than the pencil. |
| 21 | The boy is taller than the man. | 22 | The snail is faster than the dog. |
| 23 | The dog is slower than the snail. | 24 | The boy is older than the man. |

## Adjectives 11: Good, Better, Best

Frame: **The ___ is ___.** New: forms that change completely, and "more" / "the most" for long words.

| Pic | Word | Word clip | Sentence | Sentence clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | good | 01 | The first drawing is good. | 02 |
| 2 | better | 03 | The second drawing is better. | 04 |
| 3 | the best | 05 | The third drawing is the best. | 06 |
| 4 | bad | 07 | The weather is bad. | 08 |
| 5 | worse | 09 | The weather is worse. | 10 |
| 6 | the worst | 11 | The weather is the worst. | 12 |
| 7 | more beautiful | 13 | The red flower is more beautiful. | 14 |
| 8 | the most beautiful | 15 | The pink flower is the most beautiful. | 16 |

Pictures: 1 to 3 are three drawings with one, two and three stars; 4 to 6 are clouds, rain and a
storm; 7 and 8 are three flowers in a row. "first, second, third" and "beautiful" are new words.
This is the hardest lesson of the set; it could be split in two if it proves too much.
As built, each set is one drawing of three things with the one meant marked; the flowers are white, red and pink.

The page also needs 6 more clips for "Yes or no?":

|---|---|---|---|

| 19 | The third drawing is bad. | 20 | The weather is good. |
| 21 | The red flower is the most beautiful. | 22 | The pink flower is the worst. |
