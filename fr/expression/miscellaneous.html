Miscellaneous Expressions in French

Click the buttons below for a grab-bag of useful phrases: asserting you're right, agreeing enthusiastically, and describing rain that keeps starting and stopping:

"J'ai toujours raison. Tu devrais le savoir, depuis le temps."

"I'm always right. You should know that by now."



"Et comment !"

"You bet!" (default)

"Un peu, mon neveu !"

"You bet!" — playful, singsong, great for banter.

"Tu l'as dit !"

"You said it!"

"Ça, tu peux le dire !"

"You can say that again."

"Tu parles !"

⚠️ Watch out: this can mean "you bet," but it also flips sarcastic ("yeah, right!") depending on tone — a little slippery.



"Il pleut par intermittence."

"It's raining off and on / intermittently."

"Il pleut par moments."

"It's raining on and off."

"Il pleut de temps en temps."

"It rains from time to time."

"Il pleut, puis ça s'arrête, puis ça reprend."

"It rains, then stops, then starts again."
