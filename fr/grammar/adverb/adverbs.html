French grammar · Adverbs

vraiment, souvent, ici, déjà

Adverbs say how, when, where, how often and how much. The good news: they never agree with anything. The two things to learn are how the -ment ones are built and where an adverb goes, because French will not let it sit between the subject and the verb.

Chart 1: from adjective to adverb

English adds “-ly”. French adds -ment, normally to the feminine form of the adjective. Four small patterns cover nearly everything, and a handful of very common adverbs are irregular.

Chart 2: where the adverb goes

The golden rule: never between the subject and the verb. English says “I often eat here”; French says Je mange souvent ici. The coloured box is the adverb's slot.
