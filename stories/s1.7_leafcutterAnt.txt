Deep in a tropical rainforest,
hundreds of tiny ants are marching.
Look!
They are carrying big green leaves!
Some leaves are much bigger
than the ants themselves.
Where are they going?
They are going home
to their underground city!
Leafcutter ants have strong jaws.
They use their jaws
to cut leaves into small pieces.
Then they lift the pieces
and carry them home.
Some ants carry leaves
many times their own weight!
What strong little workers!
Let's follow the ants underground!
Wow! What a big city!
There are tunnels going everywhere.
The tunnels connect many rooms
called chambers.
Some chambers are for food.
Others are for babies.
What an amazing underground home!
Do leafcutter ants eat the leaves?
No, they don't!
They carry the leaves
to special chambers.
There, small worker ants
chew the leaves into tiny pieces.
They use these pieces
to grow a special fungus.
The fungus becomes their food!
The ants are very careful farmers.
They clean their fungus gardens
every day.
They remove harmful bits
and unwanted mold.
Some ants have helpful bacteria
on their bodies.
These bacteria make substances
that protect the fungus
from certain diseases.
The ants work hard
to keep their food healthy!
In Ant City,
everyone has work to do!
Large workers
help protect the colony.
Medium-sized workers
cut and carry leaves.
Tiny workers care for the fungus gardens
and young ants.
The ants work together,
day and night.
What a busy city!
Deep underground
lives the queen ant.
She is much bigger
than the workers.
Her most important job
is to lay eggs.
Tiny worker ants
take care of the eggs and baby ants.
The queen can live
for many years.
She is the mother of the colony!
Now we know
the secret of Ant City!
The ants cut leaves
and carry them underground.
They grow fungus
to make food.
They build tunnels,
care for their babies,
and protect their queen.
Every ant has an important job.
Together, these tiny creatures
build an amazing underground world!
