Plants are living things.
They need food
to live and grow.
But plants do not eat
like people or animals.
So how do plants make food?
Let's find out!
Plants need sunlight.
The Sun gives plants energy.
Leaves catch sunlight
during the day.
This sunlight helps the plant
make food.
Plants also need water.
Their roots take in water
from the soil.
Roots also take in nutrients
from the soil.
Then the water and nutrients
move up through the stem.
Plants need something
from the air too.
They take in a gas
called carbon dioxide.
Tiny openings in the leaves
let this gas come in.
Now the plant has sunlight,
water, and carbon dioxide.
It is ready to make food!
The leaves use sunlight,
water, and carbon dioxide.
Then the plant makes sugar,
which is its food.
This process
is called photosynthesis.
When plants make food,
they also make oxygen.
They release oxygen
into the air.
People and animals need oxygen
to breathe.
So plants help us too!
Plants need sunlight, water,
carbon dioxide, and nutrients,
to make food.
They get nutrients from the soil
through their roots.
That is how plants
live and grow.
