Sometimes it is bright outside.
Sometimes it is dark.
In the day,
we see the Sun.
At night, we see
the Moon and stars.
Why do we have day and night?
We live on Earth.
Earth is always moving.
It spins around and around.
This spinning
is called rotation.
The Sun shines all the time.
But only one side of Earth
faces the Sun at a time.
That side gets light.
That is daytime.
The other side of Earth
is turned away from the Sun.
That side does not get sunlight.
It is dark there.
That is nighttime.
Earth keeps turning
all day and all night.
A place that has day
will later have night.
A place that has night
will later have day.
That is because
Earth keeps spinning.
Earth takes about 24 hours
to turn one full time.
That is one whole day.
So day and night happen
again and again.
Every day,
Earth turns once.
Why do we have day and night?
Because Earth spins.
The side facing the Sun
has day.
The side turned away
has night.
Now you know!
