Write the story in exactly this format, as plain text in one code block. Chunks are natural breaks in sentences so that children can shadow the speaker.

# <Story title>
page: <file name for the web page, letters/digits/underscores, e.g. Clover_valley>
slug: <short folder name for pictures and audio, e.g. Clover>
menu: stories
brand: <the title again, with the two words or phrases to colour in [square brackets]>

## 1
EN: <chunk>
EN: <chunk>
MY: <Burmese translation of the whole sentence>

EN: <chunk>
MY: <Burmese translation of the whole sentence>

## 2
...

Rules:
- One "## N" heading per picture, numbered 1, 2, 3 … with no gaps. The
  picture files will be named 1.png, 2.png … in the same order.
- Each sentence is one or more EN lines (chunks), then exactly ONE MY line
  with the Burmese for the whole sentence. Put a blank line between sentences.
  If one English line holds two short sentences ("Oh no! Its legs were
  stuck!"), it gets one MY line.
- For fairy tales and fables, the first sentence under "## 1" is the title
  on its own. Science stories have no title sentence.
- A fable's moral ends the last picture: "Moral:" is its own chunk.
- Chunks are short phrases of about 2–6 words, split at natural pauses:
  before "and"/"but", after a fronted phrase ("One day," / "At last,"),
  between a quote and its speech tag ('"Go down,"' / "said the magician.").
  Short sentences stay one chunk. Never more than about 12 words in a chunk.
- Use straight quotes (" and ') in the English.
- Burmese: written style ending in …ပါသည်။ for statements, spoken style for
  exclamations (ဘယ်လောက် လှပတဲ့ အတွေးလဲ!). Where a sentence introduces a
  science term, keep the English term with the Burmese in brackets the first
  time, e.g. resin (သစ်စေး); after that, the Burmese alone.
- New words at the end: one picture per word, with the heading
  "## N | New Words", one EN line (the word) and one MY line each.
- menu is "stories" for fairy tales and other stories, "aesop" for Aesop's
  fables, "science" for science readers.
- Nothing else: no notes, no picture prompts, no extra lines.
