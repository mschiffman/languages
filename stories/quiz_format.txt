How to write a quiz.md for a mixed vocabulary quiz (six formats, Alpine.js).
Save it in Desktop\story-md and type its name in the story app (Process), or run:
    python -E stories/new_quiz.py <that file>

============================================================================
A. A quiz for a lesson that already exists
   (its words, pictures and audio are reused: nothing new to make)
============================================================================

# At School: Mixed Quiz
type: quiz
page: voc_atSchool_quiz_mix
from: voc_atSchool
menu: ytstory

## quiz
pick: 5
listen: 5
match: 2 x 4
article: 4
spell: 4, max 10
type: 4

## similar
a pen, a pencil, a crayon, a colored pencil, a highlighter
a desk, a chair

## also
a piece of paper = a sheet of paper
a colored pencil = a coloured pencil

============================================================================
B. A quiz for a new set of words (no lesson page)
============================================================================

# Fruit: Quiz
type: quiz
page: voc_fruit_quiz_mix
slug: voc-fruit
menu: ytstory

## words
1 | apple | an apple
2 | banana | a banana
3 | grapes | a bunch of grapes
4 | orange | an orange

## quiz
pick: 4
listen: 4
match: 1 x 4
type: 4

============================================================================
Every line
============================================================================

Header (before the first "## " line)
  # <title>     The page title and the menu title. The header of the quiz shows
                it without ": Quiz" / ": Mixed Quiz".
  type: quiz    Tells the story app this is a quiz, not a story.
  page:         File name of the quiz page, without .html.
  from:         A: the lesson page whose words to use (stories/<from>.html).
  slug:         B: folder name for the pictures (img/ytstory/<slug>/) and audio.
  menu:         Optional. ytstory, science, stories ... adds the quiz to that menu:
                after its lesson (A), or at the end of the Vocabulary group (B).
  group:        Optional (B). Another menu group than Vocabulary.
  pictures:     Optional (B). Folder in G:\My Drive\_YOUTUBE with the hi-res
                1.png, 2.png ... when it isn't named like the slug.
  size:         Optional (B). Longest side of the converted pictures in pixels
                (size: 800). Left out: 80% of the PNG, like the lessons.
  audio:        Optional (B). Clip folder under eng/yt when it isn't the slug,
                to point at clips that already exist.

## quiz     Optional. Left out: pick 5, listen 5, match 2 x 4, article 4, spell 4, type 4.
  One line per block; the quiz asks the blocks in this order.
    pick: 5              picture -> choose the word (4 options)
    listen: 5            hear the word -> tap the right picture (4 pictures)
    match: 2 x 4         2 questions, each 4 pictures to match with 4 words (2 to 6 pairs)
    article: 4           picture + "___ eraser" -> a or an
    spell: 4, max 10     picture -> tap the letters in order; words up to 10 letters
    type: 4              picture -> type "a book"
  The number is how many questions. The words are picked at random on every play.
  To choose the words yourself, list them instead of the number:
    pick: a desk, a chair, a pen
    match: a pen, a pencil, a crayon, a highlighter      (one question with these pairs)
  The same format can appear more than once.

## similar  Optional. One line per group of words that are easy to mix up.
            They are used as each other's wrong options, which makes pick and
            listen harder. Without it, the wrong options are random.

## also     Optional. Other spellings marked right in the typing questions:
            <answer> = <other form>, <another form>

## words    B only. One line per word:
              <picture number> | <word> | <phrase>
            - picture number: N for N.png / N.jpg
            - word: the bare word ("apple"): spelled in "spell", and what a/an goes with
            - phrase: what the learner sees and types ("an apple", "a bunch of grapes").
              Optional: without it the word is used everywhere.
            The clips are numbered in the order of the lines, the word first, then
            the phrase: 01 apple, 02 an apple, 03 banana, 04 a banana ...
            You don't write clip numbers or file names. To use clips that already
            exist, name their folder with "audio:" in the header and keep the words
            in the same order as the clips; for a lesson's clips, use "from:" (A).

Pictures (B): put the hi-res 1.png, 2.png ... in G:\My Drive\_YOUTUBE\<slug> (the
story app's step 0 does that from Downloads). new_quiz.py converts the ones that
are missing to small JPGs in img/ytstory/<slug>/.
Audio (B): story_audio.py makes the missing clips with ElevenLabs, exactly as for
a story (the story app's step 2). Clips that already exist are not made again.

Rules the script checks: every word in quiz / similar / also is a word of the
quiz; there are enough words for each block (article needs "a/an + word"
phrases, spell needs words short enough); pick and listen need 4 words or more.
